Welcome

Quiet, private, woodland-edge.

The Reindeer Lodge is the newest of the four, a Scandi-feeling timber lodge built two years ago at the woodland edge, where the deer occasionally come down at dusk. Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, and an open-plan kitchen with double-height windows looking straight into the trees.

The private wood-fired hot tub sits on covered decking outside the lodge, with views across the farm fields. The king-size master bedroom has an ensuite and direct deck access. A wood-burning stove anchors the open-plan living area.

Sleeps up to 6 across the king-size master, a twin bedroom, and a king-size sofa bed in the living area. Two well-mannered dogs are welcome with a small pet fee.

House rules.

The essentials to keep things a pleasure for everyone who stays.

Times and capacity
  • Check-in after 3pm
  • Check-out before 10am
  • 6 guests maximum. Only guests on the booking may stay overnight.
During your stay
  • No parties or events
  • Quiet hours after 10pm
  • No smoking or vaping indoors. Outside is fine.
  • Remove outdoor shoes at the door
On departure
  • Strip the beds and leave linens in one place
  • Put used towels in the bath or shower
  • Wash any dishes you've used
  • Dispose of rubbish in the right bins
  • If anything's broken or not working, just message us. No judgement.

Safety and property.

A few things about the setting that are worth knowing before you arrive.

The setting
  • Reindeer Lodge sits on a working farm. Animals graze freely in the surrounding fields, so please don't feed or touch them.
  • There's a large fishing lake nearby that isn't fenced. Be mindful when walking through the fields, especially with small children.
  • Wear appropriate footwear. Fields and lanes can be muddy in any season.
The hot tub
  • Wood-fired, on covered decking, and yours alone.
  • In winter it can take up to 6 hours to heat. Keep the lid on while heating and feed the fire every 20 minutes.
Safety equipment
  • Smoke alarms throughout
  • Carbon monoxide alarm
  • Fire extinguisher

Good to know.

Practical bits to help your stay run smoothly.

What we leave you
  • Toilet rolls, washing-up liquid, dish sponge and cleaning cloth
  • Dishwasher tablets and laundry tablets
  • Tea and coffee-making facilities ready for arrival
  • Bed linens, towels, hair dryer and iron all provided
Logs and fuel
  • We provide enough logs for one hot tub session and one log burner session.
  • Top-ups are available at the Esso garage, about 10 minutes' drive away.
Pets
  • Pets are welcome with a pet fee per stay (covers extra cleaning).
  • Please keep dogs off beds and furniture.
  • Please clean up after them and ensure they're well-behaved and house-trained.
Arrival and access
  • Self check-in via a secure lockbox. Arrive on your own schedule, no key handover required.
  • Full private access to the entire property for your stay.
  • Parking is directly outside the lodge.
  • After booking you'll receive a secure portal link with the lockbox code, check-in details, and local recommendations.
  • Early or late check-in can sometimes be arranged. Just ask when you book.

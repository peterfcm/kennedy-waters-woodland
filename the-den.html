Two people, two nights, nothing else asked of you.

The Den is a small-family farm stay for up to 3 guests, with a double bed in the main bedroom and a single bed hidden in a magical loft den that children love. Designed by Lia Gold of the BBC's Interior Design Masters with Alan Carr.

There's a wood burner that takes the chill off in seconds, and a small kitchenette with everything you need for breakfast and not much more. The window opens directly to the lower field, sheep in spring, fog in autumn.

Best at the edges of the year. Late autumn for the bath and the burner, early spring for the lambs.

House rules.

The essentials to keep things a pleasure for everyone who stays.

Times and capacity
  • Check-in after 3pm
  • Check-out before 10am
  • 3 guests maximum. Only guests on the booking may stay overnight.
During your stay
  • No parties or events
  • Quiet hours after 10pm
  • No smoking or vaping indoors. Outside is fine.
  • Remove outdoor shoes at the door
On departure
  • Strip the beds and leave linens in one place
  • Put used towels in the bath or shower
  • Wash any dishes you've used
  • Dispose of rubbish in the right bins
  • If anything's broken or not working, just message us. No judgement.

Safety and property.

A few things about the setting that are worth knowing before you arrive.

The setting
  • The Den sits on a working farm. Animals graze freely in the surrounding fields, so please don't feed or touch them.
  • There's a large fishing lake nearby that isn't fenced. Be mindful when walking through the fields, especially with small children.
  • Wear appropriate footwear. Fields and lanes can be muddy in any season.
Safety equipment
  • Smoke alarms throughout
  • Carbon monoxide alarm
  • Fire extinguisher

Good to know.

Practical bits to help your stay run smoothly.

What we leave you
  • Toilet rolls, washing-up liquid, dish sponge and cleaning cloth
  • Dishwasher tablets and laundry tablets
  • Tea and coffee-making facilities ready for arrival

Arrival and access
  • Self check-in via a secure lockbox. Arrive on your own schedule, no key handover required.
  • Full private access to the entire property for your stay.
  • Free on-site parking for 2 cars. Further parking on the gravel past the main entrance.
  • After booking you'll receive a secure portal link with the lockbox code, check-in details, and local recommendations.
  • Early or late check-in can sometimes be arranged. Just ask when you book.

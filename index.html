The setting

A corner of Staffordshire worth lingering in.

The farm sits in the village of Blithbury, between Rugeley and Uttoxeter, a fifteen-minute drive from Cannock Chase Forest and a short walk to the village pub. Birmingham is 45 minutes away, London just under three hours by train from Rugeley Trent Valley on the West Coast Main Line.
